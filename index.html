What does zero upfront mean?

Do we have to buy anything?

No. Purchase is optional. Eligible travelers must attend the live presentation together to participate in this offer.

Will there be another sales pitch during vacation?

No on-property sales presentation is required for these offers. The required presentation happens online before travel.

Does the photograph show our exact trip?

Photographs are representative. The actual property, ship, room, dates and availability are confirmed through your pass and itinerary.
