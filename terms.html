These summaries explain the offer families. Your issued Vacation Pass, its full terms and your confirmed itinerary control the actual travel arrangement. Photos are representative; listed destination options and participating cruise lines do not guarantee availability.

The presentation

Attendance at a 45–60 minute live online presentation is required for eligible travelers. Both partners attend together. There is no upfront charge to attend and purchase is optional. Qualification includes age 25+, household income of at least $75,000, a major credit card and traveling at least twice a year; final eligibility is confirmed with you. No on-property sales presentation is required.

Cruise getaways

3, 4 or 5 nights, for two adults. Travel taxes and fees are approximately $145, $151 or $157 per person for a 3-, 4- or 5-night sailing, subject to change. A $150 deposit applies after a valid itinerary is selected; refundability ends at confirmation under the pass terms. At least 45 days’ advance booking is required; June–August and major holidays are excluded.

All-inclusive escapes

5 days / 4 nights, for two adults. Taxes and fees are $195–$269 per person. A $298 deposit applies after itinerary selection. Refundability, booking windows, exclusions and availability follow your Vacation Pass terms.

Condo weeks

7 nights, for up to four. Travel taxes and fees start at $15 per night per person. A $150 deposit applies. Availability, booking windows, exclusions and refund conditions follow your Vacation Pass terms.

Resort + airfare credit

5 days / 4 nights, for two adults. Government, carrier and agency fees are approximately $150 per person. A $240 deposit applies. Airfare-credit restrictions, refund conditions and travel availability follow your Vacation Pass terms.

Reservations and availability

Do not purchase nonrefundable flights or make other commitments before receiving the confirmations required by your pass. Booking lead times, blackout periods, deposits, refunds, restrictions and expiration rules vary. Call us if you need the full terms for a particular pass before deciding.
